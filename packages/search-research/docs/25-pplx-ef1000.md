# Pplx HNSW ef_search=1000 follow-up

Measured 2026-09-06. Added the pgvector 0.8.2 maximum ef_search setting of 1000.
The original two-year slice, BF16 vectors, 196 queries, top-100 candidates,
m=16/ef_construction=128 and lexical RRF weight .125 remain fixed.

## Accuracy on the retained original graph

| ef_search | Exact top-100 recovery | Hybrid hits@8 /196 | Hybrid hits@20 /196 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Exact | 100% | 143 | 158 |
| 800 | 98.85% | 141 | 155 |
| 1000 | 99.14% | 141 | 156 |

Increasing search breadth recovers one top-20 target with no losses versus 800,
while top-8 totals are unchanged. The baseline uses the original PG exact
candidate lists, including its ordering of near ties; the new query plan confirms
HNSW index usage. Original graph and previous artifacts are preserved.

## Matched VM comparison

The temporary inference-VM experiment repeats the prior CPU/container/storage
conditions, with exact serial, HNSW 800 and HNSW 1000 only. Two randomized passes
supply 392 warm measurements per method. Automatic prepared-plan reuse remains
disabled. Index rebuild means this VM graph is evaluated separately from the
retained local graph. Timings cover dense lookup only, excluding embedding,
lexical retrieval/fusion and SSH. No production settings are changed.

| VM path | Median ms | p95 ms | Exact top-100 recovery | Hybrid hits@8 /196 | Hybrid hits@20 /196 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Exact serial | 165.35 | 170.66 | 100% | 143 | 158 |
| HNSW 800 | 11.49 | 14.14 | 98.87% | 141 | 155 |
| HNSW 1000 | 13.43 | 17.16 | 99.16% | 142 | 157 |

On this VM graph, 1000 costs about 17% more median latency than 800, recovers two
additional hybrid top-20 targets, and remains about 12.3x faster than serial
exact. The original retained graph recovers one additional target instead of
two, so do not present a single graph's small residual gap as deterministic.
Both builds favor 1000 over 800 for this bounded quality comparison. This is a
reasonable provisional high-recall setting, not proof of exact equivalence or
full-corpus/filtered behavior. Parallel exact was not rerun in this follow-up;
its previous measurement is preserved in report 24.
